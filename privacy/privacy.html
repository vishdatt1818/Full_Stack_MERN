Privacy Notice

For purposes of this Privacy Notice, “Personal Data” means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or can reasonably be associated with an identified or identifiable individual, or as otherwise defined by applicable law.

This Privacy Notice explains the types of information we collect through our website, communications, project submission tools, engineering and manufacturing services, and other services we provide (collectively, the “Services”). It also explains how we use, process, store, disclose, and protect that information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including name, company name, job title or role, business email address, telephone number, mailing or business address, information submitted through website forms, requests for quotations, project inquiries, communications with Polymer Connection, supplier or manufacturing inquiries, and other information you choose to provide.

When using certain Services, you may also provide technical, engineering, manufacturing, commercial, or project-related information as described below.

We may automatically collect certain information when you visit or interact with our website, including IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages viewed, referring website, approximate geographic information derived from an IP address, website usage and interaction information, cookies, and similar technologies.

2. Customer and Technical Information

Polymer Connection assists customers with engineering, material selection, manufacturing processes, sourcing, supplier identification, quoting, commercialization, and related activities.

In connection with these activities, customers may provide CAD and STEP files, engineering drawings, specifications, part numbers and revision information, material specifications, manufacturing requirements, quality requirements, testing requirements, production volumes, target pricing, tooling information, application information, photographs and illustrations, reports and technical documents, RFQs and quotation information, project schedules, supplier information, and other technical or commercial project information.

Some Customer and Technical Information may not constitute Personal Data but may nevertheless be confidential, proprietary, or commercially sensitive.

Polymer Connection will use Customer and Technical Information for legitimate business purposes associated with evaluating, developing, quoting, sourcing, manufacturing, testing, supporting, or commercializing the applicable project.

3. CAD Files, Drawings and Project Documentation

Customers may submit drawings, CAD files, specifications, models, photographs, and other engineering documentation through our website, email, file-transfer systems, project-management systems, or other approved methods.

Polymer Connection may process these materials for design-for-manufacturing analysis, manufacturing process evaluation, material evaluation, supplier selection, tooling evaluation, cost and quotation analysis, technical feasibility analysis, quality planning, testing, project management, and manufacturing support.

Where appropriate for the project, Polymer Connection may share relevant technical information with manufacturing partners, material suppliers, tooling suppliers, testing organizations, engineering providers, and other parties involved in evaluating or executing the project. We seek to limit such disclosure to information reasonably necessary for the applicable business or project purpose.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information we collect to provide and improve our Services; respond to inquiries; evaluate project opportunities; prepare quotations; conduct engineering and manufacturing evaluations; recommend materials and manufacturing processes; identify appropriate manufacturing partners; facilitate communications between customers and suppliers; manage active projects; provide technical and commercial support; perform quality and manufacturing-related activities; improve our website and internal systems; conduct analytics; maintain security; prevent fraud or misuse; maintain business records; comply with legal and regulatory requirements; communicate information about Polymer Connection’s capabilities and services; and conduct legitimate business development and marketing activities.

We may also use aggregated or de-identified information for internal analytics, process improvement, benchmarking, and development of our Services, provided such information does not reasonably identify a customer, individual, or confidential customer project.

5. Artificial Intelligence and Automated Processing

Polymer Connection may use artificial intelligence, machine learning, automation, and other computational technologies to assist in providing and improving our Services.

These technologies may be used for document classification, project intake, data extraction, CAD and geometry analysis, design-for-manufacturing analysis, material and manufacturing process evaluation, technical research, supplier identification, project organization, quotation analysis, report preparation, customer relationship management, internal knowledge management, and administrative automation.

Customer information may therefore be processed by automated systems and, where necessary, technology providers supporting these systems. Polymer Connection seeks to use appropriate safeguards when confidential or proprietary customer information is processed using third-party technology providers.

i
AI Model Training
Polymer Connection does not use confidential customer technical information to train publicly available artificial intelligence models.

Where automated analysis is used to support engineering, manufacturing, sourcing, or other significant project decisions, such analysis is intended to assist rather than replace appropriate professional and human judgment. Customers should not rely solely upon automated recommendations for safety-critical, regulatory, engineering, manufacturing, or other decisions requiring professional verification.

6. Manufacturing Partners, Suppliers and Other Project Participants

Polymer Connection operates as an engineering, manufacturing commercialization, sourcing, and manufacturers’ representative organization.

To perform our Services, we may share appropriate customer information with manufacturing partners, material suppliers, tooling suppliers, testing laboratories, engineering providers, quality providers, logistics providers, equipment suppliers, and other technical or commercial partners.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to evaluate, quote, develop, manufacture, test, deliver, or support a customer project. Polymer Connection does not consider the disclosure of project information to an appropriate project participant for these purposes to constitute a sale of Personal Data.

7. Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to support our business operations. These providers may include companies providing website hosting, cloud infrastructure, email, file storage, customer relationship management, project management, analytics, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and machine learning, accounting, communications, data backup, engineering software, manufacturing software, and marketing services.

These service providers may process information on our behalf as reasonably necessary to provide their services to Polymer Connection.

8. Website Analytics

We may use analytics technologies to understand how visitors use our website. These technologies may collect information such as pages viewed, time spent on pages, referring websites, browser information, device information, and IP addresses.

We use this information to understand website performance, improve content, identify technical problems, understand visitor interests, and improve our Services.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies. A cookie is a small data file stored on your browser or device when you visit a website.

We may use necessary cookies to support essential website functionality and security; functionality cookies to remember preferences and improve website functionality; performance cookies to help us understand website performance; analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website; and, where used, marketing cookies to help measure marketing activities or provide more relevant communications.

Most browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

10. Marketing and Business Communications

Polymer Connection may use business contact information to communicate with existing customers, prospective customers, suppliers, manufacturers, and other industry participants regarding project opportunities, manufacturing capabilities, materials, processes, technical information, industry information, new services, relevant Polymer Connection content, and other legitimate business communications.

Recipients may opt out of promotional email communications by using an unsubscribe mechanism provided in the communication or contacting Polymer Connection. We may continue sending non-promotional communications necessary for active projects, existing business relationships, contractual matters, security notices, or administrative purposes.

11. Data Security

Polymer Connection seeks to use commercially reasonable administrative, organizational, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect Personal Data and confidential customer information. These measures may include access controls, authentication, secure cloud services, data backups, encryption, monitoring, and other security practices where appropriate.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and Polymer Connection cannot guarantee absolute security. Customers are also responsible for using appropriate security practices when communicating with Polymer Connection and protecting their own accounts, credentials, systems, and information.

12. Data Retention

Polymer Connection may retain Personal Data and Customer and Technical Information for as long as reasonably necessary to perform Services; maintain project history; support future manufacturing requirements; maintain engineering and quality records; comply with contractual obligations; maintain legitimate business records; resolve disputes; protect legal rights; and comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Retention periods may vary depending upon the nature of the information, project, customer relationship, contractual requirements, regulatory requirements, and legitimate business needs. Information may be deleted, anonymized, or archived when it is no longer reasonably required.

13. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

This Privacy Notice addresses privacy and information-handling practices and does not replace any confidentiality agreement, nondisclosure agreement, supplier agreement, customer agreement, purchase order terms, intellectual-property agreement, or other contractual obligation between Polymer Connection and another party.

Where a separate agreement imposes additional confidentiality or information-security requirements, Polymer Connection will handle applicable information in accordance with those contractual obligations.

Ownership of customer drawings, CAD files, intellectual property, specifications, and other proprietary information is not transferred to Polymer Connection merely because such information is submitted to us.

14. Legal Disclosures

Polymer Connection may disclose information when we reasonably believe disclosure is required or permitted to comply with applicable law; respond to lawful court orders, subpoenas, or government requests; protect Polymer Connection’s legal rights; enforce agreements; investigate fraud or security incidents; protect customers, suppliers, employees, or others; or address actual or suspected illegal activity.

15. Business Transfers

If Polymer Connection is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, information may be disclosed or transferred as part of that transaction.

Any Personal Data transferred in such a transaction will remain subject to applicable legal requirements and any privacy commitments that continue to apply.

16. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website or communications may contain links to third-party websites, applications, manufacturing partners, suppliers, or other services.

Polymer Connection does not control the privacy or security practices of independent third parties. Users should review the privacy policies and terms of third-party services before providing information to them.

17. Your Privacy Rights

Depending upon where you reside and applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding your Personal Data, which may include rights to request access to Personal Data; request correction of inaccurate Personal Data; request deletion of certain Personal Data; request information regarding how Personal Data is used; object to or restrict certain processing; and opt out of certain marketing communications.

These rights may be subject to exceptions under applicable law, including situations where information must be retained for contractual, legal, security, manufacturing, quality, or legitimate business purposes.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information provided below. Polymer Connection may take reasonable steps to verify the identity of individuals making privacy requests.

18. Children’s Privacy

Polymer Connection’s Services are intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 18 through our business Services.

19. Changes to This Privacy Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice periodically to reflect changes in our business, technologies, Services, legal requirements, or information practices.

The revised Privacy Notice will be posted on our website with an updated effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through additional notices where appropriate or required by law.
